Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, filing complaints, and tracking resolution.

Is my identity 100% safe when reporting anonymously?
Yes. When you choose Anonymous Mode, CCMS strips all IP addresses, browser user-agents, and location metadata. Files uploaded are automatically scrubbed of EXIF metadata before being stored in our zero-knowledge encrypted vault.
What happens after I submit a corruption report?
Your complaint is encrypted client-side and assigned a 16-digit Complaint Key. An automated urgency triage system checks completeness and routes the case to an independent Anti-Corruption Vigilance Director within 24 hours.
What types of evidence can I attach?
You can attach documents (PDF, DOCX), audio recordings (MP3, WAV), photos (PNG, JPG), and video clips (MP4). Files are encrypted with AES-GCM-256 before reaching the server.
How do I track my complaint status without logging in?
Simply click "Track Case" in the top navigation bar or status lookup section and enter your 16-digit Complaint Key. No account registration or phone number is required.
Am I protected under national Whistleblower protection laws?
Yes. CCMS operates under full compliance with national Whistleblower Protection Directives, granting full immunity from employer retaliation, career discrimination, and legal liability.

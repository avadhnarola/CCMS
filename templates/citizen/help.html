Frequently Asked Questions

CCMS employs a zero-knowledge intake pipeline. When Anonymous Mode is chosen, your personal details (name, email, phone) are permanently decoupled from the case dossier sent to field vigilance officers. In addition, camera EXIF headers and IP connection records are scrubbed upon upload.

You can upload audio wire recordings, scanned payment slips, forged tender documents, WhatsApp/SMS chat screenshots, photographs, and video recordings. Ensure your evidence directly demonstrates the solicitation or extortion of illicit bribes.

Our greedy algorithm matches the case to an uncompromised vigilance inspector based on departmental specialization and lowest workload. The inspector is assigned strict SLA timers (24 to 72 hours) to verify evidence, summon witnesses, and execute sting operations.

Contact our 24/7 Anti-Corruption Emergency Hotline immediately at 1800-11-CCMS (2267). The Central Vigilance Directorate possesses statutory authority to deploy immediate physical security and initiate criminal witness protection proceedings.
